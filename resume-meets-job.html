01 · The problem

Why this needed to exist

Candidates often know that a role is interesting but do not know how well their resume matches, which qualifications are missing, or what to improve first.

02 · The solution

How I approached it

Resume-Meets-Job compares resume evidence against a target job, explains strengths and gaps, and turns the analysis into specific resume, interview, and learning recommendations.

03 · Product

Core capabilities

  • Job-fit evaluation
  • Strength and weakness analysis
  • Missing-skill identification
  • Tailored resume summary
  • Interview-question generation
  • Personalized learning plan
  • Actionable improvement priorities

The tool focuses on explanation rather than a single score, giving users a clear path from current qualifications to a stronger application.

04 · Architecture

How the system works

CV

Resume

Supplies the candidate’s current skills, experience, projects, and education.

JD

Target Role

Supplies required qualifications, responsibilities, and preferred capabilities.

⚙

Matching & Reasoning Engine

Normalizes both documents, compares evidence with requirements, and coordinates structured AI analysis.

✓

Career Improvement Plan

Returns fit reasoning, missing skills, resume guidance, interview questions, and a learning roadmap.

⌕

Text Extraction

Converts uploaded resume content into clean text for analysis.

↔

Requirement Mapper

Connects job requirements to supporting or missing resume evidence.

AI

OpenAI

Generates explanations and personalized recommendations from the mapped context.

UI

Streamlit

1. InputUpload a resume and provide a target job description.
2. ProcessMap candidate evidence to requirements and reason about gaps.

05 · Engineering

Challenges solved

  • Comparing unstructured documents fairly
  • Explaining gaps without inventing qualifications
  • Prioritizing recommendations by impact
  • Keeping feedback specific and actionable
  • Presenting a large analysis clearly

06 · Reflection

What I learned

I learned how to structure AI evaluation workflows so that the final advice is traceable to source documents and useful beyond a simple similarity score.

07 · Roadmap

Future improvements

  • Track improvement over resume versions
  • Include ATS formatting checks
  • Link missing skills to courses and projects
  • Export a personalized action report
